Troubleshooting OAuth & Direct Post

  • Ensure your app permissions include user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.
  • Verify your video duration complies with your account's max_video_post_duration_sec limit.
  • Disconnect or reconnect your TikTok account anytime through your TikTok App & Website Permissions settings.
