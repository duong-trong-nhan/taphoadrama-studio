Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

TapHoaDrama Studio ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Application collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you connect your social media accounts (including TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API) to edit and publish short-form videos.

1. Information We Access

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Your TikTok Open ID, display name, username, and avatar URL so the Studio clearly shows which account is authorized.
  • Publishing Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Creator metadata queried in real time (creator_info privacy options, comment/duet/stitch availability, and maximum video duration) and the video file, title, and settings you explicitly choose to upload.
  • Local Project Assets: Scripts, voiceover audio tracks, rendered MP4 clips, and OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device unless explicitly uploaded by you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your creator session securely via TikTok OAuth 2.0 with PKCE.
  • To display required disclosure, privacy controls, and creator limits before initiating any video upload or direct post to TikTok.
  • To execute video upload or Direct Post requests only after you review the preview and confirm your post settings.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or share creator data with third-party data brokers or advertisers. OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally on the creator's authorized environment solely to maintain session continuity and execute creator-approved publishing actions.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

You may revoke TapHoaDrama Studio's access at any time from your TikTok account security settings (Settings & privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions) or delete local token files on your device. To request support or data removal assistance, email taphoadrama5@gmail.com.
