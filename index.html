How Creators Use TapHoaDrama Studio

1. Script & Storyboard Editor

Creators organize their dialogue blocks, highlight key lines, select original story backgrounds, and adjust per-line Vietnamese TTS voice personas with full undo/redo revision history.

2. Audio Mix & Visual Preview

Combine multi-speaker narration, licensed/royalty-free background music, sound effects, and timed reaction overlays in 1080x1920 vertical format before rendering.

3. Compliant TikTok Publishing

Sign in securely with TikTok Login Kit, review creator limits via Content Posting API, manually choose privacy level, comment/duet/stitch permissions, and music consent before publishing.

Creator Transparency & TikTok Policy Compliance

  • Creators authenticate explicitly using official TikTok OAuth 2.0 (Login Kit) with PKCE.
  • Before uploading via Direct Post, the Studio queries live creator_info limits and displays the authorized creator nickname and avatar.
  • Privacy visibility starts unselected (no default value), interaction checkboxes (Comment, Duet, Stitch) start unchecked, and Commercial Content Disclosure is opt-in.
  • Every upload requires explicit agreement to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
